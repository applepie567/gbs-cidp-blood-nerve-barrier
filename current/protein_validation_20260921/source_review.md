# Source review

Checked on 21 September 2026.

- Associated article https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00401-025-02936-w reports nine CIDP and two control proteomic samples and 1,411 proteins. Figure 3 labels patients without a raw filename crosswalk.
- Figure 3 source: https://media.springernature.com/full/springer-static/image/art%3A10.1007%2Fs00401-025-02936-w/MediaObjects/401_2025_2936_Fig3_HTML.png
- Supplement captions: https://media.springernature.com/original/springer-static/esm/art%3A10.1007%2Fs00401-025-02936-w/MediaObjects/401_2025_2936_MOESM8_ESM.docx . The document contains captions for histology and qPCR images, with no proteomic sample or quantitative table. The extracted captions are retained in access/supplementary_captions.txt.
- The deposit https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/archive/projects/PXD056286 and ProteomeXchange record https://proteomecentral.proteomexchange.org/cgi/GetDataset?ID=PXD056286 identify the public source files used in this extraction.
- Native schemas, study fields, workflow links, dynamic column definitions, saved filter metadata and RAW header fragments are included under access. The review did not inspect every acquisition byte. Its mapping assessment is based on the public sources listed here.

The clinical mapping conclusion is limited to these inspected sources. This work does not establish that every public neuropathy dataset has been exhausted.
