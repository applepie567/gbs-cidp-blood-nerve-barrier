# Release v2.2.0, manuscript v41

This release accompanies the revised manuscript and replaces the current working materials from v2.1.0. Earlier commits, tags and Zenodo records remain available.

- Corrects CSF synthesis to include ten eligible proteins other than IL8, including IL6 and CD5. Nine pooled effects are positive and directionally concordant. CD5 is discordant and not statistically significant.
- Adds exploratory Fc component analyses, broader multiplicity correction, covariate sensitivity analyses, annotation reaggregation from 20 nerve matrices, and composition adjustment.
- Reports attenuation with correction across 80 nerve module tests (q = 0.087) and with composition adjustment (P = 0.375). These findings do not establish uniform Fc suppression, FcRn involvement, a causal mechanism or independent external replication.
- Adds a complete 37 page Word and PDF with all five main figures and three editable landscape tables embedded. The separate text manuscript remains available.
- Includes manuscript v41, five main figures, four supplementary figures, three tables, the 49 worksheet public workbook, supplementary methods, scripts and aggregate figure source tables.
- Adds source access and coverage audits with recorded new download dates and SHA256 values. No retrospective dates are invented for older inputs.

Public materials omit donor expression and clinical intermediates, individual omission rows and the private author archive. Reconstruction requirements are documented. External source files retain their original terms.
