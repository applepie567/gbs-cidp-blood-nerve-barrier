## Scope of this revision

The prose now follows the sequence from recurrence across blood cohorts to preparation-specific results and interpretation of the nerve Fc score alongside macrophage composition. Statistical results, testing families, source citations and table values are unchanged. Figure 1 includes the marker-index analysis. Figure 5 has additional symbol and colour keys. The table in Figure S5 uses three horizontal rules. Figures S6 and S7 move redundant explanatory text into their legends. All Word tables use three-line formatting.

The independent whole nerve comparison is CIDP versus vasculitic neuropathy. It supplies a separate tissue-level estimate but does not directly replicate the macrophage-specific CIDP versus CIAP comparison. The marker index can reflect both abundance and cell state. The PXD056286 export cannot support a disease comparison without reliable sample-to-diagnosis links and adequate Fc coverage. This is an unresolved validation test, not a negative result.
